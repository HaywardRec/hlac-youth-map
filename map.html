Need to talk to someone now?
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, 24/7
  • California Youth Crisis Line (ages 12–24): call or text 1-800-843-5200
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741
  • Crisis Support Services of Alameda County: 1-800-309-2131
  • Alameda County Behavioral Health (ACCESS): 1-800-491-9099
In an emergency, call 911. Free and confidential local help is on the map under Mental Health & Support.
